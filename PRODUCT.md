# Product Truth: Gidh Intraday

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Intraday discretionary & quantitative momentum traders in the Indian Equity / FnO markets (NSE/NFO).

## Product Purpose

Gidh Intraday is an institutional-grade, zero-latency quantitative market terminal and execution dashboard. It enables traders to monitor live/replay multi-timeframe volume surges, relative strength battalions, algorithmic triggers, and execute 5-slot MIS risk-managed orders directly into live brokers (Zerodha Kite).

## Positioning

Unlike bloated retail trading terminals full of bright neon colors and delayed feeds, Gidh delivers a high-density, purely monochrome, clinical quantitative command station focused strictly on speed, risk allocation (₹10k slots), and disciplined algorithmic rules.

## Operating Context

- Real-time live market feed processing (9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST) via ClickHouse / Go Read API.
- Tick-by-tick and 1-minute replay mode for retrospective session auditing.
- Fast keyboard & click execution for intraday MIS long/short positions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Single-Responsibility Modularity**: All UI modules strictly < 150 lines.
- **Pure Monochrome Tone**: Clinical greyscale palette (no vibrant blue/purple/neon distractions).
- **Tabular Figures**: Precision price & delta alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **5-Slot Margin Model**: Fixed ₹10,000 cash risk allocation slots with automated -1.50% SL stop floor.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **GIDH** (鹰眼 - Hawk Eye)
- Aesthetic: Institutional Monochrome Terminal (Bloomberg / Linear / Codex inspiration)
- Density: High information density, micro-borders (1px), subtle elevation, crisp typography.

## Product Principles

1. **Information Density without Clutter**: Maximize actionable signal-to-noise ratio.
2. **Clinical Monochrome Discipline**: Eliminate emotional visual triggers (no bright neon greens/reds; rely on signs and crisp typographic contrast).
3. **Instantaneous Execution & Zero Latency**: All interactions and updates occur without layout shift.
