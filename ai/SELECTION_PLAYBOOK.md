## 🏛️ Proven Battle Tactics & Civ Synergies

### 1. 🐎 Tactic 1: "The 20x Paladin All-In Rush (>20x RVOL)"
- **Discovery**: When an enemy base deploys 20x to 30x normal gold mining (RVOL) in the first 15–30 minutes and establishes hill control above VWAP, this is an unstoppable Imperial push that marches all game.
- **Battle Evidence**: In Match 1 (`2026-07-14`), `BIOCON` (20.5x RVOL) and `CONCOR` (30.5x RVOL) finished as the **#1 and #2 High-Score MVPs of the entire map** (+6.34% and +6.19%).
- **Rule**: Never stand in the way of a 20x Paladin rush holding VWAP; ride the flank alongside them!

### 2. 🛡️ Tactic 2: "The Sibling Flank Raid" (Sympathy Follower Breakouts)
- **Discovery**: When a pocket civ launches a massive assault (`BIOCON`), their un-walled flank sibling sitting at tech ceiling (`DIVISLAB`) provides the safest, lowest-damage base push.
- **Battle Evidence**: `DIVISLAB` broke out post-09:45 AM and marched +3.14% with only 0.09% counter-damage.

### 3. 💥 Tactic 3: "Zero-CLV Base Annihilation"
- **Discovery**: When an enemy base takes heavy battering ram hits on massive volume (RVOL > 4x) and collapses to its lowest wall health at 09:30 (CLV < 0.05), their economy collapses into full ruin.
- **Battle Evidence**: `HCLTECH` on Day 1 collapsed **-4.63% (#1 Destroyed Base)**. `ICICIGI` on Day 3 breached lower circuit (-8.55%, +6.95% raid profit).

### 4. ⚡ Tactic 4: "Fast-Castle Boom in 20-Day Trend Leaders"
- **Discovery**: When the top 20-day macro economic leader (e.g. `ATHERENERG` with +16.97% 20D trend) gaps past enemy walls and triggers high RVOL (>4x) at 09:30 AM, it generates massive multi-hour expansion.
- **Battle Evidence**: In Match 2 (`2026-07-15`), `ATHERENERG` finished as the **#1 MVP of the entire game (+8.09%)**, pushing an extra +3.26% post-09:30!

### 5. 💣 Tactic 5: "Palisade Box Compression Breach"
- **Discovery**: When a civ turtling behind a tight 5-day box wall gets breached on 20x siege volume, it causes an instant catastrophic defeat.
- **Battle Evidence**: `PATANJALI` in Match 2 breached its 5-day box on 20.3x RVOL and **crashed -14.73% (+16.63% raid profit)**!

### 6. 🚜 Tactic 6: "The Heavy Siege / Capital Goods Formation" (`ABB` + `SIEMENS`)
- **Discovery**: When heavy industrial civs (`ABB`, `SIEMENS`, `BHEL`, `GVT&D`, `POWERINDIA`) advance together in Feudal Age, they mount devastating mid-game Castle/Imperial pushes (+5% to +9%).
- **Battle Evidence**: 
  - Match 2: `BHEL` (+4.94%), `ABB` (+4.90%), `GVT&D` (+4.54%).
  - Match 3: `ABB` erupted from +1.67% at 09:30 to **+9.61% (#1 Game MVP)**, `SIEMENS` from +1.31% to **+7.64% (#2 Game MVP)**!

### 7. 🌧️ Tactic 7: "Map Weather / Resource Starvation" (VWAP Distribution)
- **Discovery**: When >70% of civs are forced under their own Town Centers (below VWAP), aggressive long raids stall unless backed by massive idiosyncratic resources (`DIXON`), while short siege rams crush defenseless bases.
