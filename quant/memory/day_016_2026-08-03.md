## Sixteenth Memory

Day 16 delivered crucial clarity on both priority volume and short-side management:

- **Short-extension was proven**: When a damaged stock stays strictly below VWAP at the 11:00 checkpoint (`LICHSGFIN`, `GAIL`), holding past 11:00 captures sustained afternoon downside without taking unnecessary risk.
- **Priority Paladins require VWAP respect**: 10x+ RVOL provides early momentum, but if price drops below VWAP by 11:30 (`ITC`), the split-exit must trigger immediately.
- **Afternoon giveback remains an open question**: Hold-extended winners (`ABCAPITAL`) can reverse in the late session, showing that an afternoon trailing stop or VWAP-breach exit is essential.
