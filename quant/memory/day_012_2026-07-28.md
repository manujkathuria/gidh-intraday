## Twelfth Memory

Day 12 taught the first real exit upgrade:

- Lunch lockdown should block new trades, not force exits from valid winners.
- Existing winners can be held through lunch if they remain above VWAP.
- A hold rule needs invalidation; breaking VWAP turns holding into hoping.
- Group-confirmed longs work better than isolated chases.
- Real damaged shorts need more than a bad candle: they need RVOL, VWAP failure,
  and character/regime context.
- Flat breadth can hide strong long and short opportunities at the same time.

The Day 13 question:

Can we formalize the hold rule as: hold winners past 11:30 only if they are
above VWAP, still in the leading group, and not losing relative volume/character
quality?
