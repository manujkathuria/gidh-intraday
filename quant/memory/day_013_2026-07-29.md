## Thirteenth Memory

Day 13 refined the hold rule:

- Hold-rule validation cannot wait until 11:30 if VWAP breaks earlier.
- Above VWAP at entry is not enough; acceptance must persist.
- Yesterday's leading group can shrink into only a few names the next day.
- If the group is no longer dominant, sibling-flank trades become stale-leader
  trades.
- Good losses are useful when they enforce process and prevent hoping.
- Damaged-name shorts still work best in the morning window (`SUZLON`).

The Day 14 question:

Can we split exits into two rules: early invalidation on VWAP loss before 11:30,
and hold-extension only for names still above VWAP at 11:30 with group support?
