## Sixth Memory

Day 6 taught two important upgrades:

- Broad green days still have internal leadership and internal weakness.
- Sector labels are too blunt. Subgroups matter.

The market did not simply say "banks up." It said something closer to:

- PSU/public-sector banks strong.
- Select financials strong.
- Private banks weak.
- IT cooling.
- Prior sellers broadly bouncing.

This is our first clean battalion lesson. We should not only track individual
characters; we should also track which characters move together.
