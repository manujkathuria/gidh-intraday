## Fourteenth Memory

Day 14 confirmed the split-exit framework:

- Before 11:30: exit longs that lose VWAP; exit shorts that reclaim VWAP.
- At 11:30: extend only trades still accepted by VWAP and supported by a live
  group or character thesis.
- Do not treat above-VWAP red names as automatic longs; that can be
  damage-control, not strength.
- Short exits may need their own extension rule when a damaged stock remains
  below VWAP past 11:00.
- Selective auto strength returned, but breadth stayed mildly red.

The Day 15 question:

Can we add a short-extension mirror rule: hold shorts past 11:00 only if the
stock remains below VWAP, the damaged character is intact, and the broader tape
does not turn strongly risk-on?
