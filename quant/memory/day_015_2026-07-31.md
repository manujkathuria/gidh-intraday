## Fifteenth Memory

Day 15 clarified several key operating rules:

- **Priority Paladin candidates appeared**: Stocks with extreme 09:30 RVOL (>10x) and strong VWAP acceptance (`HYUNDAI`, `BAJFINANCE`) provided the cleanest opportunities on the long side.
- **Long side was cleaner than short side**: Short candidates experienced midday chop and failed to stay below VWAP into the 11:00 window.
- **Short-extension was not proven**: `LICHSGFIN` reclaimed VWAP at 10:45, meaning the short-extension rule never had a valid below-VWAP scenario to execute.
- **Short invalidation worked**: Exiting `LICHSGFIN` at 10:45 on VWAP reclaim strictly enforced risk discipline and prevented midday squeeze exposure.
- **Sieges require battalions**: Labeling `CGPOWER` as an industrial siege was a mistake; solitary momentum cannot substitute for sector confirmation.
